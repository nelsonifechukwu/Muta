Why it teaches like this

New tools aren't the answer. Curiosity is.

Most tutoring software gets faster at handing over the answer. Muta is built to slow that moment down — asking the question that makes an idea click, instead of the one that makes it memorized. It's the difference between being told a derivative is "the rate of change," and feeling it, the way a speedometer needle already makes sense to you.
