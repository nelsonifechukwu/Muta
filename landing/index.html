Make “rate of change” move.

A definition is easy to repeat. An idea is easier to keep when you can touch it. Move the point and watch the curve's steepness become a number.

Notice

At x = 2.2, the curve is rising 4.4 units for every step to the right.

That number is the derivative at that exact point — the graph's speedometer.

How Muta teaches

First intuition. Then language. Then proof.

Muta does not hide the answer forever. It earns the moment when the answer becomes useful.

  1. 01

    Start somewhere familiar

    A speedometer before a derivative. A market scale before an equation. Something the learner can already feel.

    guided discovery
  2. 02

    Ask the question that reveals the gap

    Muta listens to the explanation in the learner's own words and changes pace when the idea is still shaky.

    Socratic + Feynman
  3. 03

    Build and check each step

    Large problems become small goals. Where maths can be verified, Muta checks the working before moving on.

    subgoals + tools
