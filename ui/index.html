Your privacy, your choice

Help us see where Muta is useful.

When this laptop gets online, Muta can share pseudonymous product activity and an approximate city-level location derived from its network. This helps us count active installations and map Muta’s reach.

  • Sends a durable random installation ID, app version/build, operating-system and processor family, recent-use time, and active/registered local-user counts.
  • Never sends conversations, questions, files, voice, usernames, email, GPS, or the laptop name.
  • Your choice is saved on this laptop, so Muta will not keep asking.
  • You can change this in Settings. Turning it off also asks the server to delete this installation’s history.
