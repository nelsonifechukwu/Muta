## Narrow conservative exclusion cluster

Adopted decision identifier: `finite_membership_one_free_count_v1`. This is an
**admission-risk cluster**, not a claim that all its members are isomorphic full
reasoning graphs or a replacement for actual source family metadata.

An item belongs to this cluster when all these properties hold:

1. Its unknowns are nonnegative integer membership-region counts for two or
   three overlapping binary attributes in a finite population.
2. The supplied population total and partial marginal/intersection counts,
   optionally with an explicit ratio of region counts, leave one free count
   after elimination. All remaining region counts are affine functions of it.
3. Conservation, nonnegativity and integrality bound that free count. An
   unobserved outside region or an unspecified overlap is the missing joint
   information; no new stochastic mechanism or decision process is essential.
4. A requested region count or fixed-denominator probability varies over the
   feasible set. The task asks for that value, its possible range, compatible
   witness tables, or recognition/correction of an unjustified unique answer.
   A stored answer silently fixing the free count through exhaustive-union or
   independence reasoning does not make the source a distinct safe family.

M18 fits directly: one unknown intersection determines the other three cells;
their nonnegativity supplies its bounds and endpoint witnesses. The known
three-set neighbour fits through a free singleton count linked to another
singleton by a ratio and to the outside count by conservation. It requests
unique values where more than one table fits the explicit constraints.

These are **related affine occupancy-feasibility graphs**, not identical full
tasks. M18's explicit range, endpoint construction and non-identifiability
explanation are additional targets absent from the source. They are useful
capabilities to test, but they operate on this already-exposed underdetermined
structure. Under the strict primary-family-disjoint aim, that is sufficient
reason to decline admission conservatively rather than claim those targets
prove independence. Even if later source review validates an implicit
outside-zero convention, removing that condition alone would not certify novelty.

The exclusion covers the current M18 intersection and conditional-probability
variants, resampled numbers, renamed attributes, two/three-set renderings and
MC/worked/hint/correction wrappers **when they satisfy the definition above**.
It does not automatically cover fully specified-table conditional ratios,
explicitly independent events, Punnett models, fixed single-draw probabilities,
enumerated set membership, weighted means, or generic integer-resource problems.
Those are neither rejected nor admitted by this decision. A shared arithmetic
operation or the word “insufficient” is not enough for membership in the cluster.

## Consequences and limits

- Remove M18 from the primary authoring queue;
  retain its tested abstract solver as mathematical infrastructure, not evidence
  of new test rows or family independence. No rollback or data edit is needed.
- Its conditional 20 MC + 20 written quota becomes a 40-item design shortfall,
  not permission to oversample the other families or relabel M18. All remaining
  proposals still need their own admission checks and power review.
- M18 could support a separately authorized, explicitly exposure-related
  diagnostic later. Such results would not enter the fresh primary metric or
  repair a missing primary-family count. No diagnostic is created by this note.
- Current private semantic coverage remains 43/350. No conclusion is added
  about the unread 307, the rest of the warehouse, incumbent sources or unknown
  pretraining. This is a sufficient positive-risk decision, not negative
  corpus-wide clearance or a measured contamination effect.
- A future genuinely different essential graph would need a new specification
  and independent review; more surface variation or a clean keyword scan cannot
  reverse this recommendation for the present M18 proposal.
