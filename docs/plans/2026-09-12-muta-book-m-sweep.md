must remain a natural open book. The square beneath u has linguistic significance in the Igbo
name and is not decorative or a metaphorical foothold. Preserve it with the wordmark. Exact

supplied open-book, conversation, and serif-wordmark reference. Explore a book whose lower
structure reads as M. This supersedes the previous concept plan's avoidance of book imagery.
