files. Require their four-field raw-MCQ schema, wrappers, source labels, counts,

explicit solution-count/rank/consistency language; at least two symbolic equality

rules allow chemistry or unrelated matches. Inspect actual positives separately

proof of absent implicit reasoning graphs. A saturated locator cap must be
reported and prevents claiming every positive was manually inspected.

Synthetic tests must cover all selectors, harmless confounders, escaped Unicode,
duplicate JSON keys, malformed wrappers, nonstring/empty fields, extra fields,
