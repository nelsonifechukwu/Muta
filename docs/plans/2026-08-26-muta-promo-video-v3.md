# Muta two-minute launch film — V3 plan

## Objective

Deliver a 120-second Muta launch film whose narration and visuals are locked beat-for-beat. Every spoken product claim must be proved by the corresponding live product action or a clearly labelled motion-graphic explanation at that exact moment.

## Product source of truth

- Launch the complete offline package from `desktop/build/final-packages/Muta_0.1.449_darwin-aarch64_offline/Muta.app`.
- Use the bundled Qwen2.5 1.5B tutor as the primary model and leave it selected at the end of the model-swap demonstration.
- Use the supplied voice memo only as a pronunciation reference. Muta means “learn” in Igbo.
- Treat the repository and the running product as the authority for Eco mode, Host mode, language, document, audio, and model behavior.
- Inspect the live Muta Fleet page before describing it; do not invent dashboard capabilities.

## Editorial rules

1. No burned-in captions or comment blocks. On-screen type is limited to feature labels, verified bullets, interface callouts, and the final brand line.
2. Feature labels stay on the left with safe padding; product footage stays fully visible on the right. Never crop the Muta sidebar or brand mark.
3. Dark mode is shown only during a beat whose narration concerns the active feature; changing appearance is not used as filler under another claim.
4. Use restrained Y Combinator-style launch pacing: confident narration, tactile UI sound design, a warm electronic bed, quick clean cuts, and no stock-video clichés.
5. Prefer authentic captures. Motion graphics may explain ecosystem relationships and Eco-mode reductions, but may not pretend to be product UI.
6. Use the supplied pronunciation in the narration performance. Do not reuse the user’s voice memo as public soundtrack material.

## Build and verification

- Record the packaged native launch and separate authentic feature sessions.
- Write the final narration first, generate timecoded speech, and make the edit follow those timestamps.
- Build an original music bed and mix dialogue clearly above it.
- Export H.264/AAC, 1920×1080, exactly 120.0 seconds.
- Inspect a contact sheet plus boundary frames at every scene transition.
- Verify duration, streams, resolution, audio loudness, sidebar safe area, absence of captions/comments, and visual/narration alignment before delivery.
