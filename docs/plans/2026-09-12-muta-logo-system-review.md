# Muta logo system review — 2026-09-12

## Goal

Design and compare several distinctive identity directions for Muta, then refine the strongest
into a review-only logo and application-icon system. Preserve Muta's warm forest, ivory, and
terracotta language and test the small terracotta square beneath the `u` as a meaningful learning
symbol rather than decoration.

## Constraints

- Do not change or replace any production brand asset.
- Keep all concept boards and vector sources in `docs/design-reviews/muta-logo-2026-09-12/`.
- Do not commit, push, rebuild packages, or regenerate Tauri production assets.
- Avoid generic AI, book, chat, cube, brain, spark, owl, and graduation-cap imagery.
- The recommended mark must remain identifiable at 16–32 px and work in one color.

## Work

1. Audit the current wordmark, application icon, palette, typography, and supplied reference.
2. Build three clearly different symbol/wordmark directions around foundation, progression, and
   guided dialogue.
3. Score the directions for distinctiveness, simplicity, scalability, accessibility, and brand fit.
4. Refine the winner into light, dark, monochrome, app-icon, standalone-mark, and favicon variants.
5. Render SVG concept boards to PNG and inspect the overview and 16/24/32 px exports.
6. Ask a fresh adversarial reviewer to challenge the concept, geometry, naming, and deliverable
   completeness; fix material issues before presenting.

## Review gate

Stop after presenting the review assets and recommendation. Production replacement, package
rebuilds, and rollout remain a separate approval step.
