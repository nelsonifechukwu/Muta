# Muta two-minute promotion video

**Goal:** Deliver a polished 16:9 promotional film, approximately two minutes long, that makes
Muta's purpose emotionally legible, proves the product with the real application, and connects
the tutor to UDO's larger school-to-career mission.

**Audience:** ADTC judges first; African educators, partners, and prospective supporters second.
The film assumes no knowledge of local inference, RAG, or the architecture.

**Core promise:** A student should not lose access to patient, adaptive teaching when the
internet disappears.

**Primary message:** Muta is an offline-first AI tutor for maths and science that asks before it
tells, adapts to the learner, and lets one ordinary laptop serve a classroom.

**Payoff:** Muta supports the lesson in front of a student; UDO helps that student navigate the
opportunities beyond it.

## Reference takeaways

The supplied launch videos use a small set of repeatable moves:

- open with a human problem or a provocative one-line claim, not a logo animation;
- keep narration economical and demonstrate the product as soon as the promise is clear;
- give each product capability one visible action and one short benefit;
- alternate human-scale imagery with tightly framed product UI;
- end with a concise positioning line and a held brand card.

The Muta film will combine the emotional clarity of the short whiteboard explainer with the
founder-led, proof-first pace of the product demos. It will not imitate their wording or reuse
their footage.

## Narration

Talent is everywhere. A great tutor is not. Across Africa, a student can have the ambition to
master physics, pass WASSCE, and build the future—then lose the moment the internet drops.

That is why we built Muta.

Muta is an adaptive AI tutor for maths and science that runs entirely on an ordinary laptop.
No cloud. No waiting for connectivity. No sending a learner's work away.

But Muta does more than give answers. It asks before it tells.

Type a question, speak it, or show a photo of your working. Muta breaks the problem into the
next small step, checks the maths with verified tools, and turns an explanation into graphs and
interactive visuals you can explore.

Bring your own textbook or teacher notes, and every answer can be grounded in the material your
class already trusts—with claim-level sources beside it.

Muta remembers how each learner is progressing: what they have mastered, where misconceptions
keep appearing, and what to learn next. It can switch languages, prepare students for exams such
as WASSCE, JAMB, and KCSE, and adapt its reasoning to the battery left on the machine.

Then one laptop becomes a classroom. Host mode creates a private local network so students can
connect from their phones, even with no internet. Each learner gets a private account,
persistent progress, and a fair turn with the same local tutor.

This is where Muta meets UDO. Muta helps a student understand today's lesson. UDO helps them
navigate what comes next—mentors, universities, scholarships, and careers.

From one question, to one qualification, to a life with direction.

Muta. A tutor that asks before it tells. Built offline. Built for Africa. Built so talent never
has to wait for a signal.

## Art direction

- 1920×1080, 24 fps, H.264 delivery with AAC audio.
- Existing palette: warm ivory `#faf9f5`, ink `#302d24`, terracotta `#ad4f31`, deep green
  `#1b2a21`, and a restrained amber accent.
- Existing Muta serif/sans pairing, large editorial headlines, minimal uppercase labels.
- Product demo composition: bold feature label in the left 34%; active application crop in the
  right 66%, exactly as requested.
- Transitions: short dissolves, masked wipes, and gentle push-ins. No novelty spins, glitch
  effects, or template-like stock transitions.
- Natural Nigerian English neural voice, measured and warm; understated original ambient bed;
  captions burned in for accessibility.

## Capture and production workflow

1. Launch the real local gateway and local model; capture a short Socratic physics interaction.
2. Capture the real empty state, composer inputs, visualisation surface, sources UI, settings,
   language selector, battery optimisation, and Host mode.
3. Create only the missing human opening image; preserve the existing Muta logo unchanged.
4. Produce the narration and timed captions, then build the visual cut around the spoken rhythm.
5. Mix narration, music, and restrained transition sounds; normalize for online playback.
6. Render at 1920×1080 and inspect start, feature sequence, UDO handoff, end card, captions,
   duration, stream metadata, and representative frames before delivery.

## Acceptance checks

- duration is 115–125 seconds and the first clear promise lands within eight seconds;
- no feature claim is shown without a matching real UI state or a clearly abstract illustration;
- product demonstrations keep the requested feature-label-left/application-right format;
- Muta logo aspect ratio and colors are unchanged;
- narration is intelligible over music and does not sound rushed;
- captions remain inside title-safe bounds and have sufficient contrast;
- no network dependency is implied for Muta's core tutoring flow;
- final card connects Muta to UDO without presenting the two products as interchangeable.
