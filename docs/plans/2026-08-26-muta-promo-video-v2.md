# Muta promotion film V2 plan

## Objective

Revise the two-minute launch film around the product's broader promise: **“The personal educational companion for every African student at every level.”** The finished film must feel like a confident, modern product launch while remaining truthful to the working local application.

## Corrections from V1

- Remove the “asks before it tells” positioning everywhere.
- Rewrite the narration as one continuous story: unequal access → Muta → live product proof → a growing learner profile → classroom reach → UDO → closing promise.
- Remove all burned captions and commentary blocks. Narration will carry the story; only short, intentional feature titles remain.
- Preserve the complete Muta interface in every frame. App captures use a true 16:9 container with contain-fitting; no horizontal cover crop.
- Show both light and dark mode, using dark mode during the live product-testing section.
- Include authentic screen recording of the locally built Muta desktop app launching and being used on this Mac.
- Replace the ambient bed with an original, more propulsive launch-film track: restrained kick, synth pulse, light percussion, risers, and a resolved final lift.

## Narrative and timing

1. **0–14 s — The gap:** talent is everywhere; access to consistent help is not.
2. **14–25 s — The reveal:** introduce Muta with the new companion positioning and offline premise.
3. **25–63 s — Product proof:** launch the real app, switch into dark mode, ask a question, show step-by-step help, verified maths, and an interactive visual.
4. **63–82 s — Trusted context:** bring notes, textbooks, and past papers into the same learning flow.
5. **82–99 s — Personal growth:** show the learner profile, languages, exam preparation, and adaptive performance.
6. **99–109 s — Classroom reach:** one laptop becomes a private local learning hub through Host mode.
7. **109–115 s — Beyond the lesson:** connect learning to UDO's mentors, universities, scholarships, and careers.
8. **115–120 s — Close:** repeat the companion promise and land on “Built for how Africa learns.”

## Visual direction

- 1920×1080, 24 fps, exactly 120 seconds.
- Editorial ivory/ink/terracotta remains the core Muta look; deep forest and near-black support dark-mode sequences.
- Feature labels stay in a protected left column; the full 16:9 product window sits to the right with generous margins.
- The native launch/test recording appears as genuine motion, not a simulated cursor over still images.
- No subtitles, captions, dialogue boxes, or lower-third comments are burned into the master.
- Transitions are brief dissolves, soft directional wipes, and purposeful audio risers—no decorative effect that obscures the interface.

## Verification

- Confirm the native app process is launched from the local `.app` bundle during recording.
- Inspect representative frames at every scene boundary and during all live clips.
- Verify no left-edge UI crop and no caption layer.
- Confirm exact runtime, 1920×1080 H.264 video, AAC audio, and integrated loudness near −14 LUFS.
- Preserve V1; export V2 as a separate master and record provenance plus SHA-256.
