Keep the dot where it belongs.

Below the u in the name; below the spine when the book stands alone. Its square body remains dominant. The small tail adds dialogue without replacing the pronunciation mark.

Use the masters as a unit.

Do not retype the name, stretch the book, or add a second bubble. Leave clear space around the whole mark. Detailed minimum sizes, spacing and color guidance are in the brand guide.
